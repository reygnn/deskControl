## A. Defects

1. **A press dragged off a button and back did not count.** `ptr_motion` cleared it
   for good. Now the press is kept as the desktop it went down on (`armed`), and the
   release counts over that desktop. tests/ui.sh.
2. **rgb: took spaces, signs and 0x.** `rgb:-1/0/0` wrapped into a random colour.
   Hex digits only, as mwc's `parse_rgb_form`. tests/layout.c.
3. **Hidden workspaces got a button.** They are kept in `all_ws` and left out of
   `wss` in `mgr_done`. tests/layout.c.
4. **Right-to-left names sat at the right of their box.** Pango's auto direction
   flushed them; it is off now. tests/layout.c measures the offset.
5. **A held press moved to another desktop when the list was re-sorted.** Fixed by
   the pointer of 1. tests/layout.c.

## B. Smaller defects

6. wl_compositor below 4 is not bound, deskControl says so and exits. A wl_seat of
   any version is bound; below 3 its pointer and keyboard are destroyed, not
   released. A removed seat is let go of, the next one bound.
7. The cursor: a viewport shows an image whose size the scale does not divide at
   its size over the scale, the hotspot rounded alike; a failed theme load is tried
   again; XCURSOR_SIZE is capped at 256.
8. Colours: Mwm*foreground and Mwm*activeForeground are read; no mwmrc without HOME;
   the mwmrc is read with getline, the last line for a key wins as in mwc; dim text
   is computed by `dimmed()` to at least 4.5:1, on the amber face against amber.
9. The keymap is read by `strnlen(s, size)`; names are cut between UTF-8
   characters; `-v` and `-h` count only alone, `--help` is refused.
